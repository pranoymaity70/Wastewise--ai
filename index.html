Wet waste

Biodegradable kitchen and garden waste such as vegetable peels, leaves and flowers.

Usually: Blue bin

Keep clean and dry
⚠

Hazardous waste

Batteries, chemicals, paint, bulbs and e-waste need separate authorised collection.
